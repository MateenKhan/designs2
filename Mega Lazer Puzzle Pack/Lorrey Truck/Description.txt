Lorrey Truck with lid. I manufacture this one from 4.1mm MDF. The idea for the lid came from my 6 year old, he first instructed me to put a lid on it so I just did, when I asked why would you want to put a lid over the top (we the adults know this is not how it is in "real" life), he looked at me like I was a complete idiot and said: "dad, it´s going to rain on the sand if you don´t have a lid on it...". I have two of those boys 6 and 8 now in my RD department, doing testing and crash reports...enjoy :).
