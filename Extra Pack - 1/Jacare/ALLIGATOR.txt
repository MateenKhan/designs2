

ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS
1. Arrange the pre-stamped boards to match the diagrams.
Refer to the photographs.

2. Starting with number 1, find its corresponding piece (also number 1)

 
NOTE: Odd numbers are enclosed in black circles and even
numbers are enclosed in white squares.

3. Before assembling the pieces, smooth all the rough edges with sandpaper.

4. Join pieces by interlocking the exact two areas together that have
the same number (1 to 1, 2 to 2, 3 to 3, and so on).

5. Some joints may fit together more easily with additional sanding or
by carefully rocking the pieces back and forth while pushing them
together. NOTE: Some pieces are fragile! If a part is accidently
broken, glue can be used to repair the broken sections.

6. Use glue as necessary to strengthen the joints for display.
