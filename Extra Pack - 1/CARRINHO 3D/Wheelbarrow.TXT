	

		Wheel barrow planter (mdf)

 Wheel 1pc. 1/2" x4" x 7" glue the 2 pcs together to make the wheel.
	X is 7" Y is 4"

 bottom 1pc. 1/2" x  12" x 7"
	X is 12"

 sides  1pc. 3/8" x 8  x 11 1/2" 
	X is 11 1/2"
	   


 You also need to cut a pc of mdf 3/8" x 2" x 3 1/8" to slide in front slot

 of sides.

 To mount the wheel drill a 1/4" hole through the front forks and use a pc.

 of 1/4" wood rod as an axle.

 The Gcode is written for a 1/4" endmill. If you want to use any other size

 bit I have enclosed the dxf files.
