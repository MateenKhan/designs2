# Summary

After have a look on the website I couldn't find any Rhino model to cut on the laser so I made one myself. It is no to big but fits perfect in the shelve of my living room.

# Print Settings

Printer: Laser Cutter 
Rafts: No
Supports: No

Notes: 
For this model I have used 3mm MDF that is had around. The board was really long so you will need to fit the shapes for your laser/wood sheet. 

# Post-Printing

Because the laser I use is accurate as a potato, I have use some glue (PVA) to fit the plates in place. You may need to do this, just a tinny drop will work.

# How I Designed This

I have use a 3D model from 123D Make. It is a very useful piece of software, but kind of limited to laser cutting and CNC milling. But if you have any of this I would recommend your to try out sometime.
