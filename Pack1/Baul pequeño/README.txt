# Summary

Build your miniature vault. True, you can put some valuables inside (a stack of quarters probably), and true, you can lock the door which, when locked, has two wooden rod bars keeping it locked, but please do not rely on this vault if you do want to store valuables. Even if you glue it together, which is not needed by the way, it would not take dynamite to open it, and it will not take a truck to transport it. For decoration purposes only. And the fun of building/constructing. I had. Hope you will have too. Enjoy.

# Print Settings

Printer: K40 CNC laser cutter
Rafts: Doesn't Matter
Supports: Doesn't Matter

Notes: 
I recommend to use the PLT file. 
The model requires plywood of 4 mm thickness. 
I am cutting at a speed of 8 mm/sec with power set at 10 mAmp.

# Post-Printing

## Sanding & assembly

Laser-cutting creates a burned effect on the edges. Use a bit of sandpaper to remove and polish. Then (water color?) paint is an option. 

See attached image for assembly.
