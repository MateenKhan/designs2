[kerf_0]: Files exactly for the specified material thickness. Glue is required for assembly.
[kerf_0.15mm]: Files with cut compensation. For tight assembly (may require a hammer). Assembled without glue or with a minimum amount.

The laser burns away a portion of material when it cuts through. 
This is known as the laser kerf and ranges from 0.08mm – 1mm depending on the material type and other conditional factors.
For a tighter connection, I add a kerf of 0.15mm to all my files. This allows the product to be assembled without glue or with a minimum amount of glue.
